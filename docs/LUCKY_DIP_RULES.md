# Official Weekly Lucky Dip Promotion Rules — One Stop Gadgets

> **Promotion Format:** VIP Subscriber Exclusive Tech Giveaway (Public Viewing & Transparent Draw)  
> **Jurisdiction:** Republic of India  
> **Version:** 2.0 (Subscription-Gated Participation)  
> **Sponsor:** One Stop Gadgets (Platform Operator)

---

## 1. Eligibility & Subscription Requirement
1. **Viewing Access:** Round information, prize countdown, prize specifications, and public winners wall are open and free to all visitors and registered users.
2. **Participation & Entry Access:** Submitting an entry form for any Lucky Dip round is strictly reserved for active **VIP Membership Subscribers** of One Stop Gadgets.
3. **Non-Subscribed Users:** Non-subscribed users and guests can view the live round details and countdown, but the entry form is locked. To participate, non-subscribed users must purchase an active VIP subscription plan.
4. **General Requirements:** Entrants must be legal residents of India and aged 18 years or older at the time of entry.
5. Employees, independent contractors, deal administrators, and super administrators of One Stop Gadgets, as well as their immediate family members, are **ineligible** to participate.
6. Void in jurisdictions where promotional draws or contests of this nature are prohibited or restricted by applicable state laws.

## 2. Subscription & Entry Rules
1. **Entry Process:** Active VIP members log in, confirm their verified legal name, email, and mobile phone number, and submit their round entry.
2. **Frequency Limitation:** Strictly **one (1) entry per active VIP subscriber per weekly round**. Duplicate submissions are rejected.
3. **Membership Validity:** An active subscription must be maintained at the time of entry submission and at the time of the draw.

## 3. Provably Fair Selection Procedure
1. **Snapshot Freeze:** Exactly at the round close cutoff timestamp (Sunday 23:59:59 IST), the database freezes the eligible entrant list into a deterministic SHA-256 hash snapshot.
2. **Deterministic Random Selection:** Winner selection utilizes a Cryptographically Secure Pseudorandom Number Generator (CSPRNG, `random_bytes()`) combined with the snapshot hash. The secret entropy seed commitment is recorded before reveal.
3. **Discretionary Prohibition:** The Super Admin initiates the automated selection procedure but is technically prohibited from hand-picking or altering winners.

## 4. Winner Verification & Prize Distribution
1. Selected candidate entrants are notified via registered email and phone within 48 hours of draw completion.
2. Candidate winners must furnish government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID) to verify age (18+) and active VIP subscription.
3. If an entrant fails verification or is unreachable within 7 business days, an alternate winner is drawn using the recorded secondary seed index.
4. Prizes are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash. Sponsor covers standard domestic shipping across India.
